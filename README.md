### Featured Work

**[Campus Trust](https://github.com/Dakshified)** — Blockchain certificate verification on Algorand (PyTeal + Flask). Cut verification time from days to under 10 seconds across 300+ credentials, zero tampering incidents.

**[SahAI](https://github.com/Dakshified/SahAI)** — ML interview-assessment pipeline (Sentence Transformers, spaCy, Random Forest) for semantic answer scoring and speech evaluation. ~87% scoring accuracy across 250+ candidate responses.

**[ElectroLens](https://github.com/Dakshified/Electrolens_mobile)** — Real-time electronic component recognition app (MobileNetV2, Flutter, FastAPI, Gemini 1.5 Flash). 90.4% accuracy across 14 categories, <200ms latency, industry-sponsored.

**CAP Made Easy** — College recommendation engine (Random Forest + Reinforcement Learning) trained on 10,000+ Maharashtra CAP records, generating shortlists for 400+ students. [Published in IEEE →](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/11434285)
